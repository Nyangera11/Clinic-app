## 👥 Creating an Account

### For Patients

1. Click **"Get Started"** or **"Login"** button
2. Click **"Create New Account"**
3. Fill in your details:
   - Full Name
   - Email Address
   - Password
   - Select "Patient" as your role
4. Click **"Create Account"**
5. You'll be redirected to your Patient Dashboard

### For Health Workers

1. Click **"Login"**
2. Click **"Create New Account"**
3. Fill in your details and select **"Community Health Worker"**
4. Click **"Create Account"**
5. Access the Health Worker Portal

### For Administrators

1. Click **"Login"**
2. Create account and select **"System Administrator"**
3. Access the Admin Dashboard

---

## 🔐 Logging In

1. Go to the Login page
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click **"Log In"**
4. You'll be directed to your role-specific dashboard

### Forgot Password?

1. Click **"Forgot Password?"** on the login page
2. Enter your email address
3. Click **"Send Reset Link"**
4. Check your email for reset instructions

## 👨‍⚕️ Patient Features

### Dashboard Overview

After logging in as a patient, you'll see:

- **Welcome Banner**: Personalized greeting with your name
- **Quick Actions**: Four main services
- **Upcoming Appointments**: Your scheduled visits
- **Recent Medical Records**: Your health history
- **Health Tips**: Daily wellness advice
- **AI Chat Button**: Floating button for instant help

### Booking an Appointment

1. Click **"Book Appointment"** from dashboard
2. OR click **"Appointments"** in the header
3. Select a service:
   - General Checkup
   - Vaccination
   - Laboratory Tests
   - Maternal Care
   - And more...
4. Choose when the clinic visits your village
5. Select date and time
6. Confirm booking
7. You'll receive a confirmation

### Viewing Medical Records

1. Click **"Medical Records"** from dashboard
2. View all your health history:
   - Past visits
   - Diagnoses
   - Prescriptions
   - Test results
   - Vital signs history
3. Download or print records as needed

### Using the AI Health Assistant

1. Click the floating green button (bottom-right)
2. Type your question or click quick suggestions:
   - 📅 Book Appointment
   - 🏥 Our Services
   - 📍 Location
3. Get instant AI responses
4. Ask follow-up questions
5. Close when done

### Exploring Services

1. Click **"Our Services"** in the menu
2. Browse all available services:
   - General Checkup
   - Vaccination
   - Laboratory Tests
   - Maternal & Child Care
   - Chronic Disease Management
   - Cardiology Screening
   - Eye Examinations
   - Malaria Treatment
   - Dental Care
   - TB Screening

### Logging Out

1. Click the **Logout icon** (🚪) in the header
2. You'll be returned to the login page

## 👩‍⚕️ Health Worker Features

### Portal Dashboard

As a health worker, you have access to:

- **Patient Search**: Find patients quickly
- **Vitals Recording**: IoT device integration
- **Today's Schedule**: Your appointments
- **AI Diagnostic Assistant**: Get AI help with diagnoses

### Recording Patient Vitals

1. Search for a patient
2. Select the patient
3. Record vitals using IoT devices:
   - **Blood Pressure**: Systolic/Diastolic
   - **Temperature**: In Celsius
   - **Glucose**: Blood sugar level
   - **SpO2**: Oxygen saturation
   - **Heart Rate**: Beats per minute
4. Click **"Capture Vitals"**
5. Get AI diagnosis suggestion
6. Review and confirm
7. Save to patient record

### AI Diagnostic Assistance

After capturing vitals:
1. Click **"Get AI Diagnosis"**
2. Review AI-generated diagnosis
3. Consider recommendations
4. Add your clinical notes
5. Save to medical record

### Managing Appointments

1. View today's schedule
2. See patient queue
3. Mark appointments as complete
4. Add notes for each visit
